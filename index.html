How it works

  1. Choose your apps. Apple's Screen Time picker; Sutrah only ever sees the apps you pick, never what you do in them.
  2. At the adhan, they are held. A Sutrah shield replaces the app. Pray first.
  3. Read to open early. Tap "Open Sutrah", read Quran or do dhikr, and the app opens for the time you chose.
  4. Keep the streak. Every prayer kept and every minute read counts.

Private by design

No account. No analytics. No ads. No server. Everything Sutrah knows stays on your phone.

Location is read once, for prayer times and the qibla. Screen Time data never leaves your device; Apple applies the shield, and Sutrah cannot see your usage. Read the full privacy policy.
