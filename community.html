A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

            
Before you begin.

          

          

            

              Do I need a big following?
              

                No. You don't need a minimum follower count. Anyone who posts an
                original video about Sutrah can take part, whether you're
                sharing with a small circle or a wider community.
              

            

            

              What do I get for posting a video?
              

                You get six months of Sutrah Pro free after we verify your
                published Sutrah video. Your audience gets a three-month Sutrah
                Pro offer to use through the redemption instructions we share
                with you.
              

            

            

              How will my audience claim their free months?
              

                We'll email you the audience offer and instructions to share.
                Contact us before filming if you'd like to include those details
                in your video or caption.
              

            

            

              Does the offer renew after the free months?
              

                The App Store shows the offer's eligibility, free period and
                renewal price before redemption. For an auto-renewing offer,
                cancel in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions before the
                free period ends to avoid a charge.
              

            

            

              What should my video include?
              

                Show Sutrah or talk about your own experience using it. Publish
                your original video on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube, mention the
                audience offer, and disclose that you receive free Sutrah Pro.
                You can use a screen recording, a voiceover or a video of
                yourself.
              

            

            

              Can you use my video in ads?
              

                Sending your link does not give us permission to reuse your
                video in ads. If we'd like to feature or reuse your content,
                we'll ask you separately.
              

            

            

              Which phones can use Sutrah?
              

                Sutrah is available for iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The
                free-month offers are for Sutrah Pro.
                View Sutrah on the App Store.
